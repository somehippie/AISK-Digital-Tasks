# Facilitator materials

**Status: written, in pilot, not yet published. Expected after the first pilot run.**

Runnable session material for this curriculum exists. It is deliberately not in
this repository yet.

## Why it is held

The curriculum in [`docs/ai-starter-kit.md`](../docs/ai-starter-kit.md) is the
reasoning: why the sessions are ordered as they are, what each one is designed
to produce, and what the research says about getting that order wrong. The
facilitator packet is the other half, the part you would actually run from.

It is held because CC BY publication is a one-way door. Once templates are
published under an open licence they get copied, adapted and taught from, and
withdrawing a version that the first pilot proves wrong is not really possible.
The riskiest assumption in the whole design is that Session 1A's honest ledger
buys enough trust from a genuine skeptic to reach Session 2. That has not been
tested on anyone yet. If it fails, the templates get rewritten rather than
patched, and what gets published should be what survived a real run, not a
confident draft.

Shipping late is the cheaper mistake here.

## What lands when the pilot completes

- Session-by-session facilitator scripts, including the `[F]`-marked steps that
  depend on a live facilitator rather than a document
- The day-one baseline and week-four checkpoint as printable instruments
- The Good Time Journal template assigned at Session 2
- Whatever the first run proves needs to change, which is the point of waiting

**Session 1C is the exception, and stays one.** It was added in v0.9 as a third
entry ramp and has not been run with anyone. Its scripts will not ship with the
rest: Track A tests 1A, so a 1C pilot is a separate run that has not been
scheduled. Anyone running 1C from the curriculum alone is testing it, and
feedback on it is worth more than feedback on the two ramps that have at least
been reasoned against a real audience.

## If you want to run this before then

You can, from the curriculum document alone. Part 4 specifies every session,
its artifact, and its timing in enough detail to work from.

If you do, [pilot feedback](../CONTRIBUTING.md) is the single most valuable
contribution this project can receive, and it is what unblocks publication of
everything above. There is a
[pilot feedback issue template](../.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/pilot-feedback.md)
ready for it.
